match takes its command line and its output from fastchess
(https://github.com/Disservin/fastchess, commit 60d7a7a): the option parser (MatchArgs), the
report lines (MatchOutput) and the numbers in them (MatchStats, MatchSprt) were written from its
source so that they read and print the same. The rest - the tournament that plays the games,
its scheduling, clock, adjudication and PGN, the mapping, the scoreboard, config.json reading,
the console command - is EngineBattle's own. The documentation calls fastchess "the
reference". Its licence:
